# G-7 — Threshold Window: Leaky Summation

**Unit:** `docs/genealogy/G-07.md` · **Script:** `scripts/genealogy/g7_window.R`
**Verdict:** ⚠️ partially reproducible (channel-count ambiguity) · **Tier:** structure

## Precursor (as published)

Temporal summation in a leaky integrator — two stimuli of amplitude a within
interval Δt sum as V = a·e^(−Δt/τ) + a ≥ θ (Volkov et al. on *Dionaea*; the
standard neural two-pulse summation).

## Derivation (the precursor math)

Solve for the maximum inter-stimulus interval that still reaches threshold:

    a·e^(−Δt/τ) + a = θ
    Δt_max = τ·ln(a/(θ−a))            [single-channel form]

Our flytrap code uses the two-channel form:

    Δt_max = τ·ln(2a/(θ−a))           [two-channel form]

## Results (run `Rscript scripts/genealogy/g7_window.R`)

    τ = 8 s, a/θ = 0.952
    single-channel: 23.9 s | two-channel: 29.4 s
    published bracket 20–30 s: both inside — YES
    delta (ln2·τ): 5.5 s

## Verdict

**Partially reproducible — the instructive case.** The formula is derivable
from the published leaky-summation standard, but the source does not fix the
channel count. The 24 vs 29.5 s discrepancy in Appendix B is therefore **not
an error in our pipeline** — it is an ambiguity inherited from the
literature: the source gives a bracket, not a formula. This is exactly the
"published artifact may not be reproducible" case. The T-6 bench re-verify
can resolve it: measure the real window, let the biology pick the channel
count.
